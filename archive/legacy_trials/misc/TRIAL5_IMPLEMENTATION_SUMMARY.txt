TRIAL 5 IMPLEMENTATION SUMMARY
==============================

Trial ID
--------
TRIAL5-HSX-ROTATION-LONG-GRID

Purpose
-------
Trial 5 is not a permanent grid strategy for one stock. It models the full
commercial process:

    point-in-time ticker selection
    -> two-month long-grid deployment
    -> mandatory exit and cash settlement
    -> reselection

The test repeats this process over many historical rotations. A short live
deployment therefore still receives multi-year historical validation.

Research universe and capital
-----------------------------
Fixed HSX universe:

    FPT HPG MBB MWG PNJ SSI TCB VCB VND VPB

Initial portfolio capital is VND 100,000,000. At most two tickers are selected
per rotation. At most one ticker is selected from each frozen sector group.
Unfilled selection slots remain cash.

Walk-forward split
------------------
There are 15 chronological, non-overlapping deployment folds.

    Training:    previous 12 calendar months
    Deployment:  immediately following 2 calendar months
    First OOS:   2023-01-03 to 2023-02-28
    Last OOS:    2025-05-05 to 2025-06-30

The nine development sessions from 2025-07-01 to 2025-07-11 are unused.
The final_test period from 2025-07-14 to 2026-07-16 remains locked and is not
read numerically, selected on, simulated, or used for tuning.

Ticker selection
----------------
Selection is recomputed inside every fold using that fold's training rows
only. No global hindsight-selected ticker list is allowed.

The training year is divided backward into non-overlapping blocks containing
10 close-to-close return sessions (11 closing prices). Each block is labelled:

    deep_downtrend       return <= -8%
    oscillating_sideways ER < 0.35 and high-low range >= 1.2%
    quiet_sideways       ER < 0.35 and range < 1.2%
    uptrend              ER >= 0.35 and return > 0
    downtrend            all remaining valid blocks

A ticker must have at least 20 valid blocks, at least 35% oscillating blocks,
at most 25% downtrend plus deep-downtrend blocks, at most 5% deep-downtrend
blocks, and at least 60% oscillating plus uptrend blocks. The latest block
cannot be downtrend/deep-downtrend, and the latest six blocks cannot contain a
deep downtrend.

It must also pass:

    median daily traded value over 60 sessions >= VND 10 billion
    valid reference/corporate-action checks
    ATR20 / price <= 3%
    one 100-share lot fits exposure and stress budgets

Eligible names are ranked lexicographically, not with a fitted weighted score:
least deep-downtrend history, least total downtrend, most oscillation, largest
oscillating range, greatest liquidity, then alphabetical ticker.

Grid formula
------------
The same one-cell geometric formula is used for every selected ticker:

    A = final training close
    g = clamp(ATR20 / A, 1.5%, 3.0%)
    buy limit B = A / (1 + g)
    sell target U = A
    hard lower H = A / (1 + g)^3

Prices are rounded to modeled HSX legal ticks. There is one position per
ticker, no descending buy ladder, and no averaging down.

Position size and turnover
--------------------------
Quantity is the largest 100-share multiple satisfying:

    acquisition cash <= 15% of rotation-start portfolio NAV
    loss after three sequential 7% HSX floor moves <= 1.5% of NAV

Each floor move is rounded separately to the applicable HSX tick before the
next move. With two selected tickers, maximum initial exposure is 30% and
three-floor portfolio stress is 3%.

Before each buy, planned two-way turnover (buy notional plus target-sale
notional) is reserved. Each ticker has a hard budget of 60% of rotation-start
portfolio NAV; two tickers can reserve at most 120% per rotation.

Entry rule
----------
An order for session T uses information ending at T-1 only:

    close[T-1] > SMA50[T-1]
    10-session return > -8%
    ER10 < 0.35 OR the 10-session return is non-negative
    close[T-1] > frozen buy limit B

The latest 50 observations must have verifiable reference information and no
detected reset. The ticker account must be flat at session start.

Minute execution
----------------
Only observed continuous-session minute events can fill. Missing clock minutes
are never created or forward-filled. A normal limit fill requires:

    valid non-crossed level-one book
    spread <= 40 basis points
    displayed quantity covers the complete order
    order <= 5% of that minute's matched quantity
    best quote reaches the limit
    minute closing matched price penetrates the limit by one legal tick

The algorithm deliberately does not use intraminute high/low to invent queue
priority. It uses whole-order fills only and applies a 5-basis-point adverse
execution haircut subject to the resting limit.

Costs and settlement
--------------------
Modeled costs are:

    buy commission       0.15%
    sell commission      0.15%
    sell tax             0.10%
    execution haircut    0.05% per side
    board lot            100 shares

Exact cash is rounded to whole VND. Purchased shares become tradeable at 13:00
on the second following observed trading session. Sale cash becomes available
on the same T+2-afternoon schedule. The ledger separately tracks available
cash, pending sale cash, total quantity, tradeable quantity, and locked
quantity.

Risk management
---------------
A ticker stops accepting buys when prior-close information shows:

    10-session return <= -8%
    prior close <= hard lower H
    account drawdown from its high-water mark >= 5%

A portfolio-level 5% drawdown from the continuous high-water mark permanently
kills the strategy from the next observed session and persists across all
later rotations.

Risk exits may ignore the normal spread ceiling, but still require a valid
displayed bid, full size, and the 5% participation limit. New buys stop early
enough to settle. Scheduled liquidation begins on the third-last session.
All shares and sale cash must be settled by the final session; the simulator
never invents an end-of-period fill.

Diagnostics
-----------
The run reports:

    net return after commission, tax, and execution friction
    stitched maximum drawdown
    profitable-rotation percentage
    worst two-month return
    completed grid cycles
    realized-trade profit factor
    capital utilization
    losses realized after risk triggers while shares were settlement-locked
    performance in equal-weight ten-stock market-downtrend rotations
    buy-and-hold benchmark
    doubled commission and execution-haircut robustness

The down-market label is diagnostic only. A rotation is a broad-market
downtrend when the equal-weight ten-stock proxy loses at least 5% from the
training-end close to the deployment-end close. It never changes trades.

Decision rules
--------------
Sample sufficiency requires:

    all 15 rotations valid
    at least 10 active rotations
    at least 30 completed target campaigns

Only then are economic gates considered. They require positive compounded and
median returns, annualized two-month Sharpe >= 0.50, at least 60% profitable
rotations, trade profit factor >= 1.20, maximum drawdown no worse than -5%,
worst rotation no worse than -8%, positive doubled-cost performance,
doubled-cost profit factor >= 1.00, controlled forced-exit losses, modeled
cost <= 1% of starting NAV in every rotation, and modeled cost <= 35% of
positive pre-cost campaign profit.

Bias and leakage controls
-------------------------
The implementation:

    fixes the ten-name universe and discloses survivorship limitations
    recomputes selection only inside past training data
    freezes selected names and grid parameters for the next two months
    keeps cash/no-trade rotations in the denominator
    uses non-overlapping OOS rotations as the research sample
    filters minute rows by selected ticker and allowed date before parsing
    ignores the legacy validation_fold column
    excludes locked final-test numeric bytes from the development run identity
    requires a pre-run cryptographic seal before any authoritative simulation
    creates content-addressed outputs and a create-only decision lock

Reproducible development command
--------------------------------
From the project directory:

    python3 study_trial5_rotation_grid.py --development-walk-forward

The command has no final-test execution mode. The authoritative run is allowed
only after the source, configuration, preregistration, split files, canonical
development rows, and required minute files/manifests match the pre-run seal.

Interpretation
--------------
Fifteen two-month portfolio rotations are the statistical sample. Individual
ticker rows and trades are correlated diagnostics and must not be counted as
independent observations. Passing development would permit one separately
controlled final confirmation; it would not by itself authorize live trading.

Authoritative Trial 5 v1 result
-------------------------------
Run:

    trial5_e1f38d5856

Status:

    inconclusive_sample
    do not advance to final confirmation
    final_test used: no

The development run produced 14 valid rotations out of 15, only 6 active
rotations, and only 4 completed grid-target campaigns. The preregistered
requirements were 15 valid, at least 10 active, and at least 30 completed
campaigns.

Key observed diagnostics:

    compounded net return                 -1.4080%
    annualized two-month-fold Sharpe      -0.9745
    profitable valid rotations             14.29%
    realized-trade profit factor           0.2876
    stitched maximum drawdown              -1.7490%
    worst valid rotation                   -0.7651%
    doubled-cost compounded return         -1.5844%
    average capital utilization             0.955%
    diagnostic benchmark return            +1.5931%

Four target exits earned VND 568,359, while five forced/scheduled exits lost
VND 1,976,316. Modeled costs were VND 372,957 and consumed 50.74% of positive
pre-cost campaign profit. No realized loss was tagged to a risk trigger while
shares were settlement-locked.

The invalid rotation contained unadjusted reference resets in TCB and HPG.
Five rotations selected no ticker. In three more rotations, selected tickers
never reached the frozen qualifying buy level. When entries did occur, the
first-touch pullback rule often entered a continuing breakdown. The largest
example was an HPG gap that lost about 13.8% at campaign level despite the
prior-close hard-lower rule.

Conclusion:

    The rotation/accounting procedure worked.
    The static first-touch entry and payoff hypothesis did not.
    Trial 5 v1 is sealed and must not be repaired in place.
    Any revised entry, selector, or risk rule must be Trial 6.
